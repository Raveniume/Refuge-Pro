Refuge includes adapted component examples from AndroidLiquidGlass by Kyant0.
Source: https://github.com/Kyant0/AndroidLiquidGlass
License: Apache License, Version 2.0 (see AndroidLiquidGlass-LICENSE.txt).

Adapted examples include LiquidButton, LiquidBottomTabs, LiquidToggle and
LiquidSlider. Refuge changes include semantic theme colors, navigation state,
accessibility, native editing, touch handling, and rendering-scope integration.
