# Design Lab V3 Page Rules

- Keep the gallery component-first and compact.
- Use natural system-style labels; do not add marketing eyebrow copy or concept-shot labels.
- Keep the real profile image in the header.
- Keep Filter/Sort as quiet compact `筛选` and `排序：默认` controls.
- Show the optical test next to the real Segmented and Tab Bar components so refraction can be compared directly.
- Content examples are secondary evidence only and must remain quiet, stable, and readable.
- Dark and light screenshots are both required before a manual review.
